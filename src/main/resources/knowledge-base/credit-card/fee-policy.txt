SmartBank Fee Policy

Current fees
SmartBank is a demonstration application. In the current version, SmartBank does not charge an annual fee, a joining fee, a late payment fee or a transaction fee on credit cards or accounts.

Interest
The current version does not calculate or charge interest on the outstanding amount.

No billing cycle
The current version has no monthly statement date and no payment due date. The outstanding amount stays until the customer pays it.

Real banks
Real credit card issuers normally charge interest and fees such as late payment charges. Those charges are not part of this demonstration.
