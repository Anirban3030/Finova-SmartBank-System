SmartBank Credit Card Policy

Card types and credit limits
SmartBank offers three card types: STANDARD, GOLD and PLATINUM. The credit limit is 50,000.00 for STANDARD, 150,000.00 for GOLD and 500,000.00 for PLATINUM.

Credit limit
The credit limit is the maximum total amount a customer is allowed to spend on the card.

Available credit
Available credit is the part of the credit limit that is still free to spend. Available credit equals the credit limit minus the outstanding amount.

Outstanding amount
The outstanding amount is the total the customer currently owes on the card.

Difference between credit limit and available credit
The credit limit is fixed and does not change when the customer spends. Available credit goes down with every purchase and goes up again when the customer makes a payment.

Requesting a card
A customer can request a card of any type. A customer can hold only one card of each type at a time.

Card status
A card is INACTIVE, ACTIVE, BLOCKED, EXPIRED or CLOSED. A new card starts as INACTIVE and must be activated before it can be used. Only an INACTIVE card can be activated.

Blocking a card
A customer can block an ACTIVE card. A blocked card cannot be used for purchases and cannot be reactivated in the app.

Card validity
A new card is valid for 5 years. An expired card cannot be used. Only the last 4 digits of a card number are shown.
