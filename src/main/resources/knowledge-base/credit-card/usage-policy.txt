SmartBank Card Usage Policy

Making a purchase
A customer can make a purchase with a card by entering the merchant name (up to 100 characters) and the amount. The amount must be at least 0.01 and at most 1,000,000.00.

Rules for a purchase
The card must belong to the customer, must be ACTIVE and must not be expired. The purchase amount cannot be more than the available credit.

Effect of a purchase
A successful purchase reduces the available credit and increases the outstanding amount by the purchase amount. The purchase is saved in the card's transaction history.

Rejected purchases
A purchase is rejected if the card is INACTIVE, BLOCKED, expired, or if the available credit is too low.

Card security
Customers must never share their full card number, PIN, CVV or one-time codes with anyone. SmartBank staff and the SmartBank assistant will never ask for them.

Card history
Customers can view the card's purchase history and payment history at any time.
