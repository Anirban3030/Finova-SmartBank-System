SmartBank Account Policy

Account types
SmartBank offers two account types: Savings and Current.

Automatic Savings account
When a customer registers, SmartBank automatically creates one Savings account for them. The customer does not need to apply for it.

Optional Current account
A Current account is optional. A customer can open one later from the Accounts page.

One account of each type
A customer can hold at most one Savings account and at most one Current account. Opening a second account of the same type is not allowed.

Account numbers
Every account is given a unique 12-digit account number when it is created.

Account status
An account is always in one of three statuses: ACTIVE, BLOCKED or CLOSED. Deposits, withdrawals, transfers and card payments are only allowed on an ACTIVE account.

Account ownership
A customer can only see and operate their own accounts. Accessing another customer's account is not allowed.

Balance
The balance is the amount of money currently held in the account.
