SmartBank KYC Policy

What KYC means
KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is the process banks use to verify a customer's identity.

KYC in SmartBank
SmartBank is a demonstration application. The current version does not collect identity documents and does not run real KYC verification.

Information collected at registration
At registration, SmartBank collects the customer's name, email address, phone number of 10 to 15 digits, and a password of 8 to 64 characters.

Password protection
Passwords are never stored in plain text. SmartBank stores only a securely hashed version of the password.

Real banks
A real bank would normally verify identity documents and address before allowing full account use. That verification is outside the scope of this demonstration.
